1. Q \- How can I cancel my subscription?  
   A \- Go to your device settings \-\> Apple account \-\> Subscription \-\> Wyzly \-\> cancel subscription.  
     
2. Q \- How to connect your child to Wyzly?  
   A \- After completing the child setup on the parent device and the subscription purchase, you are reaching the QR screen. On this step you should move to your child’s device and on this step you have 2 options:  
* Enter the Wyzly app and press the child device button. After pressing that button you will have the option to scan the QR code from the parent device or to insert a code in order to connect your child device.  
* If you don’t have Wyzly app installed on your child device, please use the camera app to scan the QR. After scanning the QR, a link will appear which will open the Safari/Chrome with an option to download Wyzly app by pressing the GET button. After the app will be installed just press the OPEN button which will open Wyzly and the child will be connected.

3. Q \- Are the Notifications and Screen Time permission a must?  
   A \- Notifications permission aren’t a must but very crucial for a good experience. Wyzly using Notifications to let child knows about what’s about to happen and what’s happening.   
   Screen Time permission is a must. Wyzly is managing the screen time of the child device (following the schedule that was set by the parent) and for doing that, the screen time permission access is a must. Without that permission, Wyzly can’t manage the screen time of the device.  
     
4. Q \- What happens to the child device when the parent cancel the subscription?  
   A \- When the parent cancels the subscription, Wyzly will no longer manage the child Screen Time schedule and the child’s device will be released from any lock.   
     
5. Q \- What screen time schedule is most recommended?  
   A \- There isn’t one schedule that is more recommended than the other. It’s really up to the parent's decision. Nevertheless, we recommend the Learn & Play schedule because the schedule is already built and the parent doesn’t have to be concerned when the child’s device is locked or not. Unlike this level, The Day Pass level locks the child device first thing in the morning, and in order to release it, the child has to answer a certain number of questions (depending on the grade) in order to release the device for the rest of the day. In this level the child is more “free” in matters of device lock. The third option is the Time limit where the parent has more control on the child’s schedule. The parent can set the amount of free time the child can have by setting the scroll bar. If the parent sets less free time (1.5 for example), the child will also have less sessions of questions to answer. If the parent sets more free time (7.5 for example), the child will have more sessions to answer and more free screen time.   
   To summarize, the Learn & Play is what we recommend and what we see as standard, but it’s really up to the parent’s decision whether to set a more strict or flexible schedule.  
     
6. Q \- How can I update my child’s details like name/school/grade/date of birth?  
   A \- The child account will appear on the parent’s device home screen. After you enter/press the child details, the top rectangle with the name is editable. By pressing this section, the parent will be able to edit the child’s name, state, city, school (or home schooled), date of birth and grade.  
     
7. Q \- When I update the schedule of my child, it seems like it doesn’t work immediately and keeps the previous schedule. How can I fix that?  
   A \- There is nothing to be fixed. Schedule updates are taking effect on the next day. Please check again on the next day if you see the update.  
     
     
8. Q \- When I update the end of day time of my child, it seems like it doesn’t work immediately and keeps the previous time. How can I fix that?  
   A \- There is nothing to be fixed. Sleep time updates are taking effect on the next day. Please check again on the next day if you see the update.  
     
9. Q \- When I try to manually lock/unlock my child device, nothing happens and the child remains unlock. What should I do?  
   A \- There are couple of things you should do when you experience this issue:  
- Make sure the notifications on your child’s device are on.  
- After you set the notification to on, make sure the Wyzly app is launched on your child’s device (App is open). This will make sure the device will receive the override.

10. Q \- Is there a way to release the lock from my child’s device during a session of questions?  
    A \- Yes, you can use the parent override by “Pause Wyzly” through your child’s details on the parent device. Be aware that if you would like the lock to be back, you will need to “Resume Wyzly” through the same place. This release is also useful when the “End of day” time arrives and the child’s device is locked.   
      
11. Q \- Is there a way to answer more questions besides the ones of the session?  
    A \- Yes. After the child completes the session and releases the device, he can scroll up to see more questions. Those questions have nothing to do with the lock/unlock time and they are just for fun.  
      
12. Q \- If I delete my account, will the child account be deleted as well?  
    A \- Yes. If the parent deletes his account, all the children that are connected to the parent will be deleted as well.  
      
13. Q \- How can I delete Wyzly from my child’s device?  
    A \- In order to delete Wyzly from your child’s device you have to go to his device settings \-\> Screen Time \-\> Find Wyzly \-\> turning off screen time permission \-\> successfully delete Wyzly from his device.  
      
14. Q \- What happens if the child turns off the screen time on his device?  
    A \- If the child will turn off the screen time permission on his device, Wyzly will no longer manage his screen time schedule and the child will be able to use the device’s apps freely. An email will be sent to the parent telling him that the screen time on the child’s device was turned off and that he needs to take actions in order to return Wyzly. When Wyzly app will be open again on the child’s device, the screen time permission will be asked again.  
      
15. Q \- If I want to switch between the devices of my child but keep the account and the schedule, how can I do that?  
    A \- There is no problem to move the running child account to a different device. Please enter the child details through the parent device and press the “Pair child device” button. A QR code screen will be displayed and just scan (or use the code) in the new child’s device. After you scan the QR and connect the child, the account will be removed from the old device and will be run from the same point on the new device.
